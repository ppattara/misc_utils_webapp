Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tr7j2hdpPig
by หมอชวนฟิต [DeDoctor], uploaded 2025-08-22. Listed from the video on 2026-11-27.
Done sitting on a stable chair, with no equipment.

One exercise per line, in the video's order: name | description | timing
- "hold 10 s": hold the position for 10 seconds.
- "each side, left first": do it on the left, then on the right. The side is
  the one the head or body leans or turns toward, and the hand on the head.
- "2 times": do it twice, as two sets. With "each side", each set does both
  sides.
- "26 reps x 2.3 s": 26 repetitions, each taking about 2.3 seconds.
- "alternating": each movement to one side counts as one rep (one punch, one
  knee, one arm), so 50 reps is 25 each side.
In the video, each cardio move lasts 1 minute (the first two, 30 seconds each),
done at your own pace. Their reps and seconds per rep are what the doctor did
in that time, measured from the video. The other hold times and rep counts
come from the video's on-screen counter.
